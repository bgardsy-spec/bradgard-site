Author & Rehearse
Prompter Studio

Where the talk gets built — no code, no command line.

  • Script to slides. Drop in a Word or text script; it builds the timed deck for you.
  • Images & video. Place clips right on a slide — they play in the live deck.
  • Pacing budgets. Set a time target per slide; the run is planned before you rehearse.
  • One-click handoff. Export a project that CuePilot imports as-is.
Perform Live
CuePilot

Autopilot for your talk — your confidence monitor on stage.

  • It listens. The script scrolls and the deck advances to where you actually are.
  • Live pace HUD. On pace, behind, or ahead — plus the “watch slides” that run long.
  • Phone & clicker. Scan a QR for a phone remote; any USB/BT clicker just works.
  • Timing report. Actual-vs-budget per slide after every run. Copy or export CSV.
