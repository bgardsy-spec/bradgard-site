About

Brad Gard has spent over four decades in the electrical trade, on the tools since he was fourteen. Apprentice, journeyman, foreman, business owner, safety leader, and now Training Director at IBEW Local 120.

He speaks to industry, labour, and education audiences about the three things he has spent a career living: building the next generation of skilled trades, mentorship that changes real outcomes, and a safety culture people believe in instead of just comply with. His talks work on stage because they happened in the field first.

The Keynotes

  • Earned, Not GivenFlagship Thirty-eight apprentices. One hundred and five Grade 8 students. One day that proved leadership is revealed through real responsibility, not handed out with a title.
  • From Tools to Teaching What happens when your best electricians become your worst teachers, and how to fix it before twenty years of knowledge walks out the door.
  • Zero Incidents Is a Culture Safety paperwork saves no one. Real prevention happens in a conversation you were not in the room for. You build the culture, or you wait for the incident that proves you did not.
  • The Trades in Crisis The skilled trades shortage is not a pipeline problem. It is a perception problem, and the sector has been solving the wrong one for twenty years.
  • The Arc Flash StoryNew One live panel, one fireball, and the gear he almost skipped. A first-person account of the afternoon that proves current does not care about your plans.
