Brad Gard has spent over four decades in the electrical trade, on the tools since he was fourteen. Apprentice, journeyman, foreman, business owner, project manager, safety leader, and now Training Director at IBEW Local 120. Every stage taught him the same thing: skill is earned, not given.

Today he brings that lesson to the stage. Brad speaks to industry, education, and labour audiences about the three things he has spent a career living: building the next generation of skilled trades, mentorship that changes real outcomes, and safety culture people believe in rather than just comply with.

As Training Director, he oversees apprenticeship development, safety programming, and workforce pipeline initiatives, and has designed and delivered five consecutive rounds of provincial Skills Development Fund programming. His flagship keynote, Earned, Not Given, grew out of a program he built and ran, later studied by the Minister's office. It works on stage because it happened in real life first.

In 2026 he received NETCO's national Leadership Excellence Award for contributions to apprenticeship and electrical training across Canada, recognition he credits to the apprentices and instructors he works alongside.

National Recognition

NETCO Leadership Excellence Award

Established in 2016 and presented annually at the NETCO National Training Conference, the Leadership Excellence Award recognizes outstanding contributions to apprenticeship training and continuing electrical training across Canada — the industry training professionals who, through their organizations, help promote and strengthen education and training within the electrical sector.

  • ▹Demonstrates inspirational leadership
  • ▹Supports and advances apprenticeship and industry training
  • ▹Contributes meaningfully to the success and development of electrical training programs

A NETCO honour rooted in exactly what Brad builds: it names training, but training only matters because of the people who show up for it — the apprentices who chose this trade and the instructors who keep raising the bar.
